# Design Spec: Marketing Plan Enhancement for JustMeAndGuitars

**Date:** 2026-04-21
**Topic:** TikTok "0 to 1k" Growth Strategy Enhancement
**Target:** @JustMeAndGuitars TikTok Account

## 1. Objective

Transform the existing planning spreadsheet into a specialized growth engine for reaching 1,000 followers by June 1, 2026. The strategy focuses on "Stuck Intermediate" guitarists using a "Relatable Teacher + Atmospheric Vibe" approach.

## 2. Architecture of Enhancements

### 2.1 Strategy Dashboard (🎯 Strategy Dashboard)

- **KPIs:** Add "Saves" and "Comments" as primary success metrics.
- **Brand Pillars:** Define "Atmospheric Aesthetic" and "Intermediate Shortcuts".
- **Sprint Goal:** 0 to 1,000 followers milestone.

### 2.2 Content Calendar (📅 May Calendar)

- **Structure:** 30-day "Aesthetic Progress" series.
- **Weekly Themes:**
  - Week 1: High-reach Atmospheric Loops (Original Audios).
  - Week 2: "Soulful" Technique Shortcuts for Intermediates.
  - Week 3: Song-specific "Vibe" breakdowns.
  - Week 4: Community Engagement (Video Replies to Qs).

### 2.3 Engagement Assets (📖 Story Templates)

- **Interaction Loops:** Polls for song requests, "This or That" gear/vibe choices, and Progress Check-ins.

### 2.4 Discovery Optimization (# Hashtag Strategy)

- **Tiered System:**
  - Broad: #GuitarTikTok, #Musician
  - Niche: #GuitarTutorial, #NeoSoulGuitar, #IntermediateGuitar
  - Community: #JustMeAndGuitars, #30DayAestheticProgress

## 3. Implementation Details

- **Tooling:** Python with `openpyxl` within a virtual environment.
- **Persistence:** Direct modification of `JustMeAndGuitar_ENHANCED_Plan_May2026.xlsx`.
- **Validation:** Visual check of spreadsheet formatting and content consistency.

## 4. Testing & Success Criteria

- **Verification:** All 30 days of the calendar populated with high-signal content ideas.
- **Alignment:** Hashtags and story templates directly support the 0-1k growth funnel.
