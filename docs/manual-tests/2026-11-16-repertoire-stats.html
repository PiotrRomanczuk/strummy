Repertoire statistics (SNG-13) — manual test

Date: 2026-11-16 · Branch: feature/repertoire-stats (stacked on #1106) · Tested on: the local Supabase stack on the Mac (StrummyLocal), admin account.

What was done

A new admin page /dashboard/admin/repertoire-stats (menu: Analytics → Repertoire Stats) over every live song, its song_analyses row and its song_transcriptions stats (never the raw notes). Four views, switched through the URL (?view=, ?compare=, ?song=), so any view can be linked:

View What it shows
Data coverage Tiles: songs, with a recording link, with an analysis, with a transcription; then every song missing any of them
Songs Sortable, filterable table: tempo and key (Strummy / measured), beats per bar, bass notes per bar, bass and vocal range, data flags; up to three songs side by side
Strummy vs recording Only disagreements: tempo (half/double told apart), key (a shape key under the capo counts as agreeing; relative key named), length, chords (entered shapes transposed by the capo against the heard chords, flagged under 60%)
Song vs repertoire Histograms of tempo, key, highest and lowest vocal note, bass density; a chosen song marked in each with its percentile

Also: the backfill's key and duration rules moved from scripts/backfill/ to lib/music-theory/recording-agreement.ts (now shared with the page) with a new tempoAgreement; transposeKey is exported from harmony-analysis.ts. Charts are hand-drawn SVG like the rest of the app: neutral bars (--ink-4), the chosen song in --ink with a “this song” marker (never colour alone), validated for separation and 3:1 contrast in light and dark; each bin shows its count on hover.

Results on real data (local stack, copy of dev)

Tiles 468 / 374 / 424 / 9 — equal to the SQL counts on the same database. 258 of 424 measured songs disagree with their recording in at least one way.

How to test manually

Step Expected
Sign in as admin, open Analytics → Repertoire Stats Coverage tiles; numbers match select count(*) on songs / links / analyses / transcriptions
Songs: type part of a title, pick a category, click a column header “n of N songs” follows the filter; sorting toggles
Click “Compare” on two songs, then on a fourth after three Side-by-side panel; the oldest of four drops out; the URL can be shared
Strummy vs recording: open “Edit song” on one entry, fix the tempo, come back The tempo line is gone for that song
Song vs repertoire: pick a song, Show “this song” marker on its bin in every chart, percentile line above each
Sign in as a teacher, open the URL Sent to /dashboard

Known limits

  • Chord ID results (song_analysis_sources, #1005) are not used yet — the chord comparison uses song_analyses.chords.
  • Vocal and bass charts cover only transcribed songs (9 today).
  • Tempo distribution uses the measured tempo, else the entered one; a half/double-tempo song sits in its measured bin.
