Song map readability (#1242)

Date: 2026-11-27 · Branch: fix/song-map-readability · Scope: song page (/dashboard/songs/<id>), song card, admin repertoire stats

What changed

  1. Repeated parts are grouped. Back-to-back parts with the same name (or letter, when unnamed) share one chip: Hotel California's 24 parts become 8 chips — Intro ×4, Verse ×4, Chorus ×2, Verse ×3, Chorus ×2, Bridge ×2, Chorus ×2, Solo ×5. A chip's width still follows its bars, but it never shrinks below its text, and the strip wraps at phone width. Under the strip, a Repeat 1 2 3 4 row steps through each repeat of the picked run; the detail panel (bars, BPM, chords, drums, bass) shows the chosen repeat.
  2. One Roman-numeral convention. Every numeral counts from the song's own tonic in its own mode; a minor key reads natural minor (i ii° III iv v VI VII). Only chords outside the scale get a flat or sharp (Neapolitan bII, Dorian #VI, leading-tone #vii°). song2logic writes minor songs against the parallel major (bIII, bVI, bVII); those are rewritten at the read boundary. All numerals go through degreeNumeral in lib/music-theory/roman-numeral.ts: chordToRomanNumeral (theory card, key detection, chord analytics) and tonicNumeral (song map, song card degrees and loops, admin repertoire progressions and sections).
  3. No empty "Sections & progressions" card under the map. When the song has an analysed map and no hand-written sections, the card is gone; staff who can edit keep a single Add Section button. With sections, or without a map, the card is unchanged (its inline styles moved to Tailwind classes).
