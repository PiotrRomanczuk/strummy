Song card, step 1: card model and the teacher's card page (SNG-17) — manual test

Date: 2026-11-23 · Branch: feature/song-card-model → dev · Closes #1161 · part of #1137 · verified on a scratch Supabase stack on the Mac (migrations + seed, songlab analyses of two songs loaded by hand), not on dev — dev was unreachable (off-LAN).

What was done

  • Card model (lib/music-theory/song-card.ts, pure): one recording's analysis as bars in seconds (grid.downbeats_s, or every bar's first beat in an older analysis), the chord of each half bar, the parts of song_segments over them (name, number among parts of its kind, group = kind + loop, degrees, drums, bass bar by bar), the guitar's strum pattern, the capo advice and the YouTube id.
  • Clock helpers (song-card-clock.ts): a moment in seconds → bar, sixteenth, half, part; the chord there (teacher corrections win); the next change and how many bars away; the default strum rule (down on eighths, up between).
  • Query getSongCard: reads only the drawn JSON paths of song_analyses.analysis plus song_segments. Parts are used only when they come from the same recording as the analysis (same YouTube video in any link form); a failing or misfitting row never breaks the page.
  • Page /dashboard/songs/[id]/card (staff; a student is sent back to the song page until #1166): header with the song's facts and the analysis date, Song map (parts in proportion to their length, a timeline with a tick per bar, keyboard ←/→ by bar) and the current part's bars as a chord chart (two chords when the bar changes mid-way, repeats drawn quieter, degrees and the loop for the teacher). A click on a part, bar or the timeline moves the card's position — the YouTube clock comes in #1163.
  • Link „Otwórz kartę utworu →” on the existing Mapa utworu card of the song page.

Design: Claude Design — Karta utworu w Strummy (accepted 2026-11-23).

How to test

  1. Have a song with song_analyses (and ideally song_segments) on the stack you run against; sign in as the teacher test account.
  2. Open the song page → Akordy i struktura → Mapa utworu → „Otwórz kartę utworu →”, or go to /dashboard/songs/<id>/card.
  3. Expect: title, artist, key / tempo / metre / length / parts; „Analiza nagrania · <date>”; the parts strip (verse blue, chorus gold, intro grey), the first part pressed and its bars below, bar 1 framed in gold.
  4. Click Refren 1: its bars (e.g. F G C Am ×3), „Układ IV–V–I–vi ×3”, degrees in the bar corners. Click a bar: it gets the gold frame and a playhead line.
  5. Click on the timeline under the parts: the strip and the chart jump to that moment. Focus the timeline (Tab) and press → / ←: one bar per press.
  6. Edge cases: a song whose segments come from another recording, or have none → „Analiza części jeszcze nie przeszła…”, chart pages of 16 bars („Takty 1–16 z 89”); a song without analysis → „Nagranie tej piosenki nie ma jeszcze analizy” and a way back; a student opening the URL → the song page; a phone (390 px) → parts wrap three per row, two bars per row, no sideways scroll.
