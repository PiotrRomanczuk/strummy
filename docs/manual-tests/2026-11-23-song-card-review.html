What was done

  • Table song_card_reviews (additive): one row per song — the recording it belongs to, the approved groups of parts, a strum per group (16 marks d/u/m/.) and a chord per corrected bar. RLS: staff read and write; students nothing directly (their read comes in #1166). Anonymisation rule: all columns kept. Types regenerated.
  • Approval: above the part's bars the teacher sees „Propozycja z analizy” (60–65% major/minor agreement) with „Zatwierdź akordy” — for the whole group (one kind on one loop: all three choruses at once) — and „Popraw”. Approved: „✓ Akordy zatwierdzone — uczeń je widzi”, „Popraw”, „Cofnij zatwierdzenie”. The map marks every part „akordy ✓ / ?”.
  • Correcting chords: in „Popraw” a click on a bar steps its chord through the song's chords (back to the analysed one removes the correction); corrected chords are gold everywhere — now, next, shapes, grid and backing.
  • Strum per part (owner decision 2026-11-23): „Popraw bicie” turns the strum row into 16 buttons (↓ → ↑ → × → rest); the correction holds for the part's group, „Wróć do zasady” drops it. The backing plays the corrected strum.
  • Capo hint for the teacher in the header: the analysis' suggestion (e.g. fret 5: G · C · D · Em instead of C · F · G · Am).
  • Every change shows at once and is saved by a server action (zod-validated, staff only, RLS again); a failed save puts the stored version back and says so.

How to test

  1. Apply the migration to the stack you test on (dev: docker exec -i supabase_db_StrummyDevelopment psql -U postgres -d postgres < supabase/migrations/20261123200000_song_card_reviews.sql on uwh).
  2. As a teacher open a card, click Refren 1: the proposal panel names all three choruses. „Zatwierdź akordy” → the panel says approved, the map shows „akordy ✓” on all choruses. Reload: still approved.
  3. „Popraw”, click bar 26 (Am): it becomes F in gold; „Teraz” and the grid follow; click until Am again — the correction is gone.
  4. „Popraw bicie”, click the second sixteenth: ↓ appears; the note says you corrected the part; „Wróć do zasady” restores the rule. Play the backing: it strums your version.
  5. Edge cases: a student cannot call the action (forbidden) and reads no row (RLS test); a stored review of another recording is ignored; an invalid strum or chord is refused.
