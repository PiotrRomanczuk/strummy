Each owned table: the owner reads and writes their row, a second user of the same role neither sees nor changes it nor writes in the owner's name, a lower role cannot write. Shared tables without an owner (skills, song_of_the_week, song_sections, spotify_matches, system_logs) pin who may read and who may write. Every suite deletes its own rows in afterAll.

What the real database showed

  • Two write paths of the theory tables are wider than intended. They are pinned with it.failing; details are with the owner.
  • song_sections is narrower than intended: the write policies still identify staff the pre-split way, so a teacher created today cannot add a section. Pinned with it.failing.
  • Everything else behaves as the policies say.
