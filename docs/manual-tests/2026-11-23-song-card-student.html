Song card, step 6: the student's view (SNG-17) — manual test

Date: 2026-11-23 · Branch: feature/song-card-student → stacked on feature/song-card-review (#1173) · Closes #1166 · Closes #1137 · migration 20261123210000_song_card_for_student · verified on the scratch Supabase stack on the Mac (E2E and RLS suites run there); not yet applied to dev.

What was done

  • Function song_card_for_student(song_id) (security definer, additive): answers only for a song in the caller's own repertoire (staff: any song) and returns the card cut to what a student plays from — bars, chords per bar, key, tempo, the guitar's strum, parts with names, times, chords and loop, and the teacher's review. Left out: degrees, bass, drums, stem levels, other tracks' rhythm, the capo advice. The three analysis tables stay staff-only.
  • Card page for students: no redirect any more; a student opens the card of a repertoire song, otherwise „Ta karta nie jest jeszcze gotowa”.
  • Student view: no practice link, no degrees, no approval marks, no bass or drums. Chords of a part the teacher has not approved yet are greyed with a dotted line (owner decision 2026-11-23) — the large chord too — with „Nauczyciel jeszcze nie sprawdził akordów tej części — mogą się różnić od nagrania.” A strum the teacher corrected says „Bicie od nauczyciela.”
  • Song page: the „Karta utworu” button shows for a student whose repertoire has the song.

How to test

  1. Apply both migrations of the stack to the database you test on (dev: on uwh, 20261123200000 then 20261123210000).
  2. Put an analysed song into the test student's repertoire (a lesson with the song, or a student_repertoire row on dev).
  3. As the student open the song page → „Karta utworu”: the card plays like the teacher's, without the teacher's material; parts not approved show greyed chords and the note; approved parts (approve one as the teacher first) show plain chords.
  4. As the student open /dashboard/songs/<id>/card of a song outside the repertoire: „Ta karta nie jest jeszcze gotowa”, nothing of the analysis is sent.
