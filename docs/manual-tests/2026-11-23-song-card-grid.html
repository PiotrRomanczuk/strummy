Song card, step 2: chord now, shapes and the bar's sixteenth grid (SNG-17) — manual test

Date: 2026-11-23 · Branch: feature/song-card-grid → stacked on feature/song-card-model (#1167) · Closes #1162 · part of #1137 · verified on the scratch Supabase stack on the Mac (CCR „Have You Ever Seen The Rain” with its songlab analysis).

What was done

  • Now card: the chord sounding at the card's position in large type (read out politely to screen readers), the next change and how far away it is („Potem, za takt”), and the shapes of the current part's chords from CHORD_VOICINGS (the playing one framed in gold; a chord without a stored voicing shows its name and „brak chwytu”).
  • Bar grid („Takt N”): 16 sixteenths counted 1 e i a; the chord on its first sixteenth and again mid-bar when it changes; the guitar strum from analysis.rhythm with the default direction (↓ on the eighths, ↑ between — the analysis hears hits, not direction), weak hits greyed. The playing sixteenth is tinted.
  • Teacher only, under „Bas i perkusja · przybliżone”: the bass of this very bar (each note as long as it sounds, name without octave) and hi-hat / snare / kick of the part, plus the groove in words. Toms, crash and ride are left out — the analysis hears them in every bar.
  • Every row has a screen-reader read-out; the drum and groove labels are shared with the existing song map card.

How to test

  1. Open /dashboard/songs/<id>/card as a teacher for a song with an analysis.
  2. Expect on load: „Teraz · Intro”, the first chord in large, „Potem, za takt” and the next chord, the intro's shapes with the current one framed.
  3. Click Refren 1, then bar 24: large G, „Potem, za takt C”; shapes F G C Am with G framed; „Takt 24”: G at 1, eight ↓ on the eighths, bass G notes, hi-hat / snare on 2 and 4 / kick, „Groove części: stopa synkopowana · werbel na 2 i 4 · Hi-hat: ćwierćnuty…”.
  4. Click a bar whose chord changes mid-bar (any in Wonderwall): two chords in the grid's chord row (at 1 and at 3).
  5. Edge cases: an analysis without a guitar pattern → „Ta analiza nie ma jeszcze wzoru bicia gitary.”; a part without bass or drums → those rows are simply not there; a phone (390 px) → the grid fits the card without scrolling.
