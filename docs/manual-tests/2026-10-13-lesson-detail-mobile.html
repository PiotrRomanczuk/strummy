Lesson detail on phones: every section, in the viewer's language

2026-10-13 · branch fix/walkthrough-review-7-lesson-detail

What changed

  • The phone lesson detail always shows Songs, Notes and Assignments. Empty sections say so ("Brak utworów…", "Brak zadań…") instead of disappearing; a teacher gets "+ Dodaj utwór" (native picker over the song library) and "+ Dodaj" (new assignment for this student) in the section headers.
  • The student's level under their name is translated instead of the raw value.
  • Lesson date/time helpers take the app locale: date block "PAŹ 17 SOB", 24-hour clock "10:00", list day separators "17 PAŹ · SOB, 2026", side panel "sob. 17 paź 2026", student home countdown time. English output is unchanged.

How to test

  1. Dev stack with demo data, Polish, 390 px. As the teacher open /dashboard/lessons: separators and times in Polish.
  2. Open a lesson with songs: Songs with stage bars, Notes, Assignments · 0 with an empty line, "+ Dodaj utwór" and "+ Dodaj" in the headers.
  3. Tap "+ Dodaj utwór", pick a song: it appears in the list after refresh. Tap "+ Dodaj": new-assignment form with the student preselected.
  4. Open a lesson with no songs: the Songs section stays, with "Brak utworów dołączonych do tej lekcji."
  5. Sign in as the demo student and open the same lesson: same sections, no add controls.
  6. Desktop 1440 px: lessons table and the side panel show Polish dates.
