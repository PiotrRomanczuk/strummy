Song Detail Redesign

2026-08-12 — audio player, Chords & structure / Lyrics tabs, redesigned sections card, Quick Assign sidebar widget, header actions (Duplicate / Assign to student). Tablature explicitly out of scope.

What changed

The song detail page (/dashboard/songs/[id]) was redesigned to match the Figma/Fable "Song Detail (Standalone)" mockup, minus the tablature view:

  • Audio player — a real <audio> element (play/pause, elapsed/duration, seek, 0.75×/1× speed, loop toggle) appears under the hero whenever songs.audio_files has at least one URL; otherwise nothing renders. No waveform rendering (would need client-side audio decoding — out of scope).
  • Chords & structure / Lyrics tabs — a new always-visible switcher (independent of the existing staff-only Overview/Production tabs) splits the chords+sections card from the lyrics+notes cards.
  • Sections & progressions — the existing sections list moved into the Chords & structure tab and now shows each section's chords as inline badges instead of a plain-text blob, matching the mockup. Staff-only add/edit still works via the existing modal.
  • Quick assign — a new staff-only sidebar widget: pick students (filterable checkbox list), a due date, a goal, and "Assign to N students" — writes to student_repertoire (two new columns: due_date, goal_text). Duplicate assignments are silently skipped (upsert + ignoreDuplicates), not an error.
  • Header actions — staff-only "Duplicate" (clones the song + its sections as a draft) and "+ Assign to student" (jumps to the Quick Assign widget via a plain #quick-assign anchor) buttons next to the existing Edit link.

How to manually test

Setup

  • Dev stack running on uwh (StudentDevelopment); role accounts from CLAUDE.local.md.
  • npm run dev, sign in as Teacher, open any song from /dashboard/songs.

Chords & structure / Lyrics tabs

Step Expected
Load a song detail page "Chords & structure" tab is active by default, showing the chords grid and the "Sections & progressions" card (chords as pill badges, not a text blob).
Click "Lyrics" Switches to the lyrics + notes cards; chords/sections hide. Works identically for Teacher, Student, and Admin (not role-gated).

Audio player

Step Expected
Open a song with no audio_files No player renders — no empty/broken UI.
Open a song with an audio_files entry Player renders under the hero. Click play — button flips to pause, elapsed time counts up. Click the speed button — toggles 1×/0.75× and audibly changes pitch/rate. Click Loop — toggles on/off (playback repeats from the start at the end when on).

Quick assign (Teacher/Admin only)

Step Expected
As Student, open any song No Quick Assign card, no Duplicate/Assign buttons in the header.
As Teacher, click "+ Assign to student" in the header Page scrolls to the Quick Assign card in the sidebar.
Search/select 1+ students, pick a due date and type a goal, click "Assign to N students" Success toast ("Assigned to N students"); form resets. Re-selecting an already-assigned student and submitting again does not error — that student is just silently skipped (upsert, not a failure).
Sign in as the assigned Student, go to /dashboard/repertoire The song appears in their repertoire.

Duplicate (Teacher/Admin only)

Step Expected
As Teacher, click "Duplicate" on a song's detail page Redirects to a new song titled "Copy of <original title>", created as a draft (is_draft: true), with the same sections cloned onto the new song id.
As Student, open a song page No "Duplicate" button visible.

Scope trims (deliberate)

No waveform rendering — a real progress/scrub bar instead of a decorative waveform, since rendering one needs client-side audio decoding.
Loop is a simple whole-track on/off toggle, not the mockup's A↔B region markers — there's no UI/data model for arbitrary loop points, and building one wasn't worth it for this pass.
Sections show chords as badges but no bar-count — song_sections has no such column.
