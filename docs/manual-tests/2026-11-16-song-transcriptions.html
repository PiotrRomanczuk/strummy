Song transcriptions (SNG-12) — manual test

Date: 2026-11-16 · Branch: feature/song-transcriptions · Tested on: the local Supabase stack on the Mac (StrummyLocal). Not applied to dev or production.

What was done

  • Table song_transcriptions (migration 20261116090000): one row per song with every note song2logic study heard in the stems of the song's recording (vocals, guitar, bass, piano, other, drum parts), the bar grid with the recording's real downbeats and per-track stats. Staff read (is_admin_or_teacher()), only the service role writes.
  • Loader scripts/backfill/2026-11-song-transcriptions.ts: reads a song2logic strummy-notes run directory, --validateOnly / --commit, --remote for any non-local host. Skips deleted songs and songs linked to another video than the transcribed one (the timings belong to one recording).
  • Staff-only card in “Chords & structure”: a piano roll of the bass line or the vocal melody, eight bars at a time. Notes are placed from seconds through the real downbeats, not a steady tempo — real bars drift (in 82 of 156 analysed songs at least one bar is >25% off). A track without notes cannot be picked; the card is gone when neither track has notes.

Known limits

  • Notes that end before bar 1 (a pickup before the first downbeat) are not drawn.
  • Machine transcription: wrong octaves and bleed from other instruments happen; the card says so.
  • Only 9 of ~360 repertoire recordings have notes today; the rest arrive as study runs.
