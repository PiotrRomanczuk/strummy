Manual test report · 2026-07-29

Lesson repertoire: searchable song picker

Branch feature/lesson-song-picker · surfaces: /dashboard/lessons/new and /dashboard/lessons/[id]/edit, section III · PLAN → Songs & notes.

What changed

Before. The repertoire field was a native <select multiple> 120 px tall listing all ~130 songs as bare Title — Author lines. Adding a second song required ⌘/Ctrl-click; a stray plain click silently wiped the whole selection. Once you scrolled, you could not see what was picked, there was no way to search, and it was effectively unusable on a phone.
After. A purpose-built picker in the form's own visual language:
  • Search box on top — filters by title and artist, matches every typed word in any order, ignores case and diacritics (zolte finds Żółte Kalendarze). Title matches rank above artist-only matches.
  • Chips above the search show the current selection at all times, each with an × to remove, plus Clear all. The selection stays visible while the list below is filtered.
  • Rows are click-to-toggle with a checkbox, the title in the editorial serif, the artist underneath and the difficulty level on the right. No modifier keys, no accidental wipe-out.
  • Keyboard: ↑/↓ move the highlight (with scroll follow), ⏎ toggles it, Esc clears the query. ⏎ is always swallowed so it can never submit the lesson form mid-pick.
  • Counter line: “2 selected · 3 of 129 shown”, plus the section badge (2/129) that was already there.
  • Accessibility: real listbox / option semantics with aria-multiselectable, aria-selected per row and aria-activedescendant tracking the keyboard cursor. The “Repertoire” label points at the search box.
The song query now also returns level, so rows can show Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced. The level badge hides below 480 px to give titles the room.

Prerequisites

  • Dev Supabase stack StudentDevelopment up on uwh (192.168.1.75:55321).
  • npm run dev, then sign in as admin@dev.local (or the teacher account).
  • A seeded song library — the dev stack has ~130 songs, which is what makes the old control painful.

How to test it

Go to /dashboard/lessons/new and scroll to III · PLAN.

# Do this Expect
1 Look at the Repertoire field. Search box reading “Search 129 songs by title or artist…”, a bordered list beneath it with checkbox rows (title / artist / level), and a hint line “0 selected · ↑↓ browse · ⏎ toggle”.
2 Click any two rows. Both rows turn gold with a checked box; two chips appear above the search box; the section badge and hint both read 2; the preview rail on the right lists both titles.
3 Type zeppelin. List narrows to Led Zeppelin songs, counter reads “… · 3 of 129 shown”, and the two chips stay put even though their rows may be filtered out.
4 Press ↓ then ⏎. The second result toggles on. The form must not submit — you stay on /dashboard/lessons/new, no lesson is created.
5 Press Esc (or click the × in the search box). Query clears, the full list returns scrolled to the top, selection unchanged.
6 Click a chip's × , then Clear all. That song's row unchecks; Clear all empties the selection, hides the chip row and resets the badge to 0.
7 Fill in student + date, pick two songs, submit. Lesson is created with exactly those songs attached — open the lesson detail page and check the song list.
8 Open an existing lesson's Edit page. Its saved songs are pre-selected as chips and checked rows; adding/removing and saving persists.

Edge cases worth a look

a Search zolte (no Polish diacritics). Finds Żółte Kalendarze — accent- and case-insensitive.
b Search two words that span title + artist, e.g. beatles blackbird. Still one hit — every token has to match, order does not matter.
c Search something with no hits, e.g. nirvana. List shows “No songs match “nirvana”.” — no empty box, no crash.
d Hold ↓ to the bottom of the list, then keep pressing. Highlight stops at the last row and the list scrolls to follow it.
e Resize to a phone width (or open on a phone). Chips wrap onto their own lines, level badges disappear below 480 px so titles get full width, the list still scrolls inside its own box.
f Toggle dark mode. Everything is token-driven — selected rows use the dark gold tint, chips and check marks stay legible.
g A brand-new studio with zero songs. Search box is disabled and reads “No songs to pick from”; the list shows “No songs in the library yet — add one from Songs first.”
