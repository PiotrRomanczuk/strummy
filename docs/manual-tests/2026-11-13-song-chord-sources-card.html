What was done

A staff-only card under "What the harmony is doing": the chords in Strummy against the chords heard in the recording, each difference with when it is first heard, its share of the song, whether Logic's Chord ID confirms it and which side song2logic analyze and Ultimate Guitar (Chordonomicon) take; capo and sounding-key hints; a link to edit. The card fetches its own review (lib/services/song-chord-review-queries.ts); RLS returns nothing to a student, and a failed fetch only hides the card.

How to test manually

  1. With a chord-review run loaded, open a song from the song.chords-verified queue as the teacher → the card shows both chord lists and the differences.
  2. Open a song with a capo hint (written in shapes, recording higher) → "The chords sound like the recording with a capo on fret N".
  3. Open the same song as the student → no card, no error.
