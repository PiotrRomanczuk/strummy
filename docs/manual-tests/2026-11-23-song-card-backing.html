Sound was not listened to in this run — the clock, the BPM and the source switch were checked; listen once on a real device.

What was done

  • Backing track (tone, loaded on the first play): the card's chords strummed in the card's strum (down low→high, up high→low and softer), plus a metronome with an accented downbeat, at 40–100% of the recording's tempo in steps of 5.
  • It drives the same clock: each sixteenth is placed at its spot in the recording's seconds, so the part, bar, chord now/next and the grid move exactly as with the recording; the part loop wraps it too.
  • Transport: „Nagranie | Podkład”. With the backing the tempo is a slider with „70% · 81 BPM”; with the recording it stays the player's own speeds. Switching keeps the place in the song.
  • When YouTube refuses the video (101/150), „Nagranie” is disabled and the card switches to the backing by itself; the message says the card keeps working with it.
  • A practice link may now carry a tempo from 40%.
  • Pure part with tests: lib/music-theory/song-card-backing.ts (shape → MIDI notes with a triad fallback, the next sixteenth with the loop, a sixteenth → seconds of the recording).

How to test

  1. Open the card of an analysed song, click Podkład, press play: a strummed chord on every strum mark and a click on every beat; the time and all sections move.
  2. Move the slider to 50%: it slows down at once, the read-out says 50% and the BPM.
  3. Switch on „Pętla części” in a chorus: the backing wraps to the chorus start after its last bar.
  4. Click Nagranie again: the video continues from where the backing was.
  5. A song whose video cannot be embedded: „Nagranie” is greyed, „Podkład” is on, play works.
