Practice log and song requests on the app's design

2026-10-13 · branch fix/walkthrough-review-8-practice-requests

What changed

  • Practice (student): the page now sits inside theme-strummy like every other dashboard page. Mono eyebrow + serif title, editorial cards with mono gold section labels, ink primary button, ink-selected duration chips, token-coloured history rows. Session dates follow the locale ("13 paź, 17:30").
  • Song requests (teacher): same treatment. "Requested by", "Unknown student" and the date were hard-coded English and are now translated; the green/red buttons are an ink primary and a plain secondary; the link is gold, not blue; Polish quotes around the student's note.
  • No behaviour change: logging, undo, approve and reject call the same actions.

How to test

  1. Demo student, Polish, 390 px: /dashboard/practice. Log 15 minutes on a song with a note; the session appears at the top of Historia with a Polish date; "Usuń" removes it after confirmation.
  2. Demo teacher: /dashboard/songs/requests. The pending Landslide request shows "Prośba od: Zofia Nowak · 12 paź 2026". Approve it: the card disappears and the song request is approved (check /dashboard/songs).
  3. With no pending requests: the serif empty line "Brak oczekujących próśb.".
  4. English: the same screens read in English.
