Ultimate Guitar paste importer

Date: 2026-08-06  ·  Branch: feature/song-form-ultimate-guitar-import  ·  Surface: /dashboard/songs/new and /dashboard/songs/[id]/edit

What was built

A collapsed “Paste from Ultimate Guitar” panel now sits under the Spotify accelerator on both song forms. Open it, paste a whole UG tab page into the textarea, press Parse, and it shows exactly what it recognised. Nothing touches the form until Apply to form is pressed; Discard throws the paste away.

Applying fills, when present in the paste:

Form field Comes from
Title & Author the <Title> Chords by <Artist> heading (also Tab / Ukulele Chords / Bass Tab; a (ver N) suffix is stripped)
Difficulty Difficulty: — Novice/Easy → Beginner, Intermediate/Medium → Intermediate, Advanced/Expert/Hard → Advanced
Capo (fret) Capo: — “No capo” → 0, “2nd fret” → 2; anything above fret 20 is ignored
Chords the chord list under the bare Chords heading, deduplicated, order preserved
Sections & lyrics the tab body, with the UG page chrome stripped
Ultimate Guitar link a ultimate-guitar.com URL, if the paste contains one

Per the agreed fill policy, every detected field is overwritten on apply — the preview is what makes that safe. Fields the paste says nothing about are left alone.

How the tab body is separated from the page chrome

The body starts after UG's strumming widget (the Strumming heading, the “Are these strumming patterns correct?” line, and the bare beat digits), or — if there is no widget — at the first [Section] header. Leading blank lines and any line that exactly repeats the parsed title or artist are dropped, runs of 3+ blank lines collapse to 2, and the trailing edge is trimmed. Chord-over-lyric column alignment is preserved byte-for-byte, since that alignment is the data.

A paste with no page chrome at all (just a bare tab) is treated as all body, and the chord list is then harvested from the tab's own chord-only lines instead.

Known rough edge

UG pages repeat the title and artist just above the tab. Those two lines are dropped only when they match the heading exactly. Real pages are sometimes internally inconsistent — the page used for verification spells the title one way in the heading and another way in the body — and when that happens both echo lines survive at the top of the lyrics field. Delete them by hand. This is deliberate: a fuzzy match would risk silently eating a genuine first line, and visible noise is better than silent data loss.

Other edge cases worth a look

  • A UG page with Capo: 3rd fret — should fill 3, not 0
  • A “Ukulele Chords by” or “Bass Tab by” page — heading should still resolve
  • A page whose title carries (ver 2) — the suffix should be stripped
  • A very long tab (500+ lines) — the field takes it; the preview reports the line count
  • Narrow viewport (≤480px) — the panel, preview rows and buttons wrap without overflow
  • Dark mode — the panel uses --gold-tint/--gold-dim, which have dark values

Note on scraping

Parsing runs entirely in the browser on text the user has already copied. Nothing fetches ultimate-guitar.com — UG blocks automated access, and a paste sidesteps that question entirely while being about as fast for the user.
