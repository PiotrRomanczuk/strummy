Song tempo and length: one value on the page and the card

Date: 2026-11-27 · Issue: #1241 · Branch: fix/song-tempo-length-consistency

What changed

The song page header, the song map card on that page and the song card (/dashboard/songs/<id>/card) now show one tempo, one length and one analysis date, all decided by one pure rule in lib/music-theory/song-tempo.ts:

  • Tempo. With an analysed recording the recording is the source, at the pulse a player counts (song2logic's felt tempo when it heard a half-time feel, otherwise its beat). If that is about 2× or ½× the teacher's tempo (songs.tempo, within 8%), the analysis counted the wrong pulse: the tempo is halved/doubled to the teacher's pulse and the counted beat is shown beside it ("counted at 143 BPM in the recording"). Any other gap over 25% keeps the recording's value and adds a small check mark (tooltip: both values). No recording: the teacher's tempo.
  • Length. With an analysed recording, the recording's length (it is what plays). Otherwise the song's own.
  • Date. The date of the analysis the tempo, length and bars come from (song_analyses), on both views (the map used song_segments.analysed_on, a later run).
  • The beat grid is untouched: bars are the recording's downbeats in seconds and the backing track still runs at the counted beat, so the card stays in sync. The backing read-out ("70% · 50 BPM") now multiplies the shown tempo.
  • A student (RLS hides the analysis from them) sees the song's own tempo and length on the song page, as before.

How to test

  1. npm run dev against the dev stack; sign in as the dev teacher (defaults in playwright.config.ts).
  2. Open /dashboard/songs/f39f14ef-4035-4a9c-926d-8ab5aa3adac0 (Hotel California). Expected: Tempo 73 BPM, Length 6:44 (hover: "From the analysed recording"); the song map says 73 BPM and "Analysed recording (Nov 10, 2026)".
  3. Open its card (…/card). Expected: Tempo 73 BPM, Length 6:44, pill "Recording analysis · Nov 10, 2026".
  4. Open /dashboard/songs/55bd07e1-ac60-4cf7-80b2-ce99a7075d6f (Nothing Else Matters). Expected: 71 BPM with "counted at 143 BPM in the recording", length 6:25; the map parts show 71 BPM. On the card: same tempo; switch to the backing track, slide to 50%: read-out "50% · 36 BPM", and the bars still move with the music.
  5. Edge: a song whose teacher tempo is far off (e.g. "Mury", 120 vs 158): a check mark next to 158 BPM, with both values in its tooltip.
  6. Edge: a song without analysis, or signed in as a student: the song's own tempo and length, no counted note, no mark.
