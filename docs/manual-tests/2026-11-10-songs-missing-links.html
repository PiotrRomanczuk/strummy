Missing song links queue — manual test

Date: 2026-11-10 · Branch: feature/songs-missing-links · Tested on: local Supabase stack on the Mac (copy of the dev database), Chromium via Playwright, signed in as teacher@dev.local.

What was done

  • New staff page /dashboard/songs/links, "Songs without a recording": every live song with no YouTube link, with a YouTube search link for it, its Spotify link and length when known, and a field to paste the video's link.
  • Two buttons per song, "Exercise" and "Other", for entries that have no recording. They set the song's category to Ćwiczenia or Inne. Category is free text, so both show up as tabs on the songs list with no schema change, and the entry leaves the queue.
  • Only a link to a single YouTube video is accepted (watch?v=, youtu.be/, /shorts/); a search page, a channel or another site is refused in the form and again on the server.
  • A "Missing links" button for teachers and admins in the songs list header. Students are redirected away from the page.

How to test it yourself

  1. source .local-stack/env.sh && npm run dev, sign in as teacher@dev.local, open Songs → "Missing links".
  2. Click "Search on YouTube" on a row, copy the address of the right video, paste it, "Save link". The row goes away and the song page shows the link.
  3. Click "Exercise" or "Other" on an entry that has no recording. It goes away, and the songs list gets that tab.
  4. Sign in as student@dev.local and open /dashboard/songs/links: you land on the songs list.

Not checked, and limits

  • Step 4 of "how to test" (student redirect) and the Polish texts were not opened in the browser; the redirect follows the same code as the song-requests page.
  • "Exercise" and "Other" overwrite the song's previous category (a genre such as "Folk").
  • The page does not search YouTube itself or check that the video is the right song; it opens a search in a new tab.
  • Saving a link does not start an analysis. The next song2logic batch picks the song up.
  • The whole queue is one page (94 rows today), without paging or search.
