Song card, step 3: the YouTube recording as the card's clock (SNG-17) — manual test

Date: 2026-11-23 · Branch: feature/song-card-player → stacked on feature/song-card-grid (#1168) · Closes #1163 · part of #1137 · verified in headless Chromium against the local dev server (scratch Supabase stack on the Mac), with the real YouTube player.

What was done

  • Player: the analysed recording embedded from YouTube (react-player v3, privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com) — the only sound of the card. While it plays the card follows it (one read per frame): playhead, part, bar, chord now / next, the bar grid.
  • Transport (sticky under the top bar): play/pause, time, „Refren 1 · takt 25”, tempo from the speeds the player itself offers (50 / 75 / 100% for YouTube; a player with fine steps is cut to 50 / 60 / 75 / 90 / 100), „Pętla części” (the current part repeats; clicking another part moves the loop), and for the teacher „Link do ćwiczenia” — copies a link that opens the card on that part, with the loop and tempo.
  • Deep link: ?part=<n>&loop=1&tempo=75 opens the card there (homework, #1139).
  • Recording unavailable: when YouTube answers 100 / 101 / 150 (removed, private, embedding forbidden by the owner), the player is replaced by a message and „Otwórz na YouTube ↗”; play is disabled, the card still works by clicking.
  • Song page: a „Karta utworu” button in the hero (staff), next to „Edytuj”, for every song — the card says itself when there is no analysis.
  • CSP: frame-src was 'none'; it now allows only https://www.youtube-nocookie.com, and script-src adds https://www.youtube.com (the IFrame Player API). Who may frame Strummy is unchanged.

Two traps found on the way and handled in useYouTubeClock: react-player's onReady rides on the element's first loadstart, dispatched before React listens (readiness uses loadedmetadata); the element reports buffering after a seek as play, which started the song on load (the clock listens to playing). Its error event does not reach React's onError, so the player node gets its own listener.

How to test

  1. As a teacher open the song page of an analysed song → „Karta utworu” in the hero.
  2. Press play: the video plays, the time runs, the part / bar / chord follow the music. Press 75%: the video slows down, the card keeps up.
  3. Click Refren 1, switch on „Pętla części”, click its last bar and play: at the end of the chorus it jumps back to its start.
  4. „Link do ćwiczenia” → paste the link in a new tab: the card opens on that part, loop on, same tempo.
  5. Open /dashboard/songs/<id>/card?part=4&loop=1&tempo=75 directly: Refren 1, loop on, 75% selected once the player is ready; nothing plays until play is pressed.
  6. Edge cases: a video whose owner forbids embedding → „Nagranie niedostępne” with the YouTube link; a song with no analysis → the card's empty message (button still there); a phone → no sideways scroll.
