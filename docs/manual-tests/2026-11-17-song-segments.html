Song segments + song map card (SNG-16) — manual test

Date: 2026-11-17 · Branch: feature/song-segments (on feature/song-suggestions, #1117) · verified on the local stack on the Mac (StrummyLocal), not on dev.

What was done

  • Table song_segments (migration 20261117090000): one row per song with the recording part by part — parts (name, repeat, bars, chords with degrees and their loop, bass riff, drum groove, stem levels), bass_bars (the bass of every bar, kept apart) and the song's key, tempo, feel and metre. Staff read, only the service role writes.
  • Loader scripts/backfill/2026-11-song-segments.ts: reads a song2logic strummy-segments run directory; skips deleted songs and songs linked to another video; refuses to write to a non-local database without --remote.
  • "Song map" card on the song page, staff only, above the transcription card: the form as a strip (a part's width is its bar count, its stripe its kind), and for the picked part the chords bar by bar with degrees (the loop and its repeat count when the part loops), the drums as a sixteenth grid with the groove in words, and the bass riff — with note names only when an average bar plays at least 60% of it.
