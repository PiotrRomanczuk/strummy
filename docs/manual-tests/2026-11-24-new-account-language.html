New account keeps the language of the invitation; student onboarding in Polish — manual test

What was done

  • The language of a new account. profiles.locale defaults to en and outranks the browser language on every dashboard request, so a student invited by a Polish teacher read the invitation in Polish and then got the whole app in English. Accepting an invitation now saves the language that page was shown in. If that write fails, the claim still completes.
  • Student onboarding is translated. The rail, the “guitar journey” step, the buttons and the final screen read from a new Onboarding message namespace (English and Polish). The answers are still stored as keys, not labels.
  • One step count. The journey step said “STEP 2” beside a rail that said “Step 1 of 2”. Both now come from the same numbers.
  • No invented promise. The summary under the goals used to say the first song would be “Blackbird” and the lessons would open with fingerpicking, whatever was picked. It now says the teacher sees the answers before the first lesson, which is true (they show on the student card).
  • Language in Settings → Profile. The only switch was in the sidebar’s account menu. The profile card now has a Language field that applies at once.

Not in this change: the teacher-only onboarding steps (About you, Your studio, Schedule) keep their English body text — nobody can reach them today, because teachers do not self-register. Accounts that already exist keep whatever locale they have.

How to test

Prerequisites: the app running against the dev stack, mail going to the dev Mailpit (SMTP_HOST set — otherwise GMAIL_* in .env.local sends real mail), a browser whose first language is Polish.

#StepExpected
1Sign in as the dev teacher, /dashboard/users/new, add a student with a fresh @dev.local address.Student card opens; an invitation arrives in Mailpit.
2In a private window with Polish as the browser language, open the link from the mail and set a password.The invitation page is in Polish; after submitting you land on /onboarding.
3Look at the onboarding screen.Everything in Polish. Rail footer and eyebrow both say “Krok 1 z 2”. “Zakończ” is disabled.
4Pick a goal.The summary says the teacher will see the answers; no “Blackbird”. “Zakończ” is enabled.
5Click “Zakończ”.Dashboard opens in Polish, navigation and content.
6Check the row: select locale from profiles where email = '…'.pl.
7/dashboard/settings → Język → English.The page re-renders in English without a reload; choosing Polski brings Polish back. Reload keeps the choice.
8Repeat steps 1–5 with an English browser.Everything in English, locale stays en.
9Settings at 375 px width.The Language field fits; no horizontal scroll.

Steps 1–7 and 9 were run on 2026-11-24 against StrummyDevelopment with a throw-away student, deleted afterwards. Step 8 is covered by the component tests only.
